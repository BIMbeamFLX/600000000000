Am I joining a cult?+

No. You’re finding the 600 Billion community on Nostr. The stones are real. The solemn ceremony is mostly a button.

What does connecting do?+

It asks your NIP-07 browser signer for your public Nostr key. The connection stays in this tab. It does not register membership, publish a note, or claim tokens.

How much is the welcome?+

The proposed first-cohort amount is 0.021 whole 600 tokens, with smaller amounts for later cohorts. Only 6,000 whole tokens were issued, so 21 each cannot cover 600,000 members. The schedule needs to be finalized before claims open. Your Nostr identity and a Liquid receiving address will be needed; the welcome is once per member.

On Nostr. Start with the crew’s Nostr profiles. Pebble membership does not include the private Signal group or other inner-circle access.
